## 1. Core Novelty & Defensibility Statement

The centerpiece of this patent disclosure is **Layer 5 (Adaptive Policy Constraint Synthesizer)**. Using the **Interchangeability Test**:
- If QAOA is replaced with classical ILP or Greedy solvers, the invention remains intact.
- If Federated Learning is replaced with another detector, the invention remains intact.
- **If Layer 5 is removed**, the system collapses into a generic mathematical solver with zero domain safety, causing illegal physical actions and operational decision oscillation.

Layer 5 is the computer-implemented mechanism that dynamically transforms live threat probabilities, confidence tiers, business SLAs, statutory policy rules (e.g. **45 CFR § 164.312(a)(1)** Access Control Safeguards, **DPDP Act, 2023**, and **IT Act, 2000 Section 43A**), and historical EMA feedback into a multi-factor Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) constraint matrix.

## 3. Recommended Patent Claim Structure (Form 2 Complete Specification)

### Independent Claim 1 (Method Claim)
A computer-implemented method for real-time multi-objective security incident response optimization in heterogeneous cloud-IoT networks, comprising:
1. Receiving calibrated threat detection probabilities generated by localized neural network edge detectors;
2. Aggregating multi-factor context parameters including C-I-A asset business criticality, service level agreement (SLA) priority, and statutory compliance tags under the DPDP Act, 2023;
3. Dynamically synthesizing an adaptive constraint matrix defining resource-specific feasible action sets, forbidden action sets, an operational budget ceiling, and an action switching penalty $\lambda_{\text{switch}}$ that penalizes mitigation action changes between consecutive evaluation rounds;
4. Formulating a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) objective function $H(x)$ balancing containment effectiveness, operational downtime cost, business impact, and action switching penalties;
5. Solving the QUBO model via an interchangeable optimization engine to output an actionable binary mitigation vector $x^*$; and
6. Executing the optimal incident response playbook corresponding to binary vector $x^*$.

### Key Dependent & Independent Claims (2 - 10)
- **Claim 2 (System Claim)**: Autonomous cloud security incident response system architecture.
- **Claim 3 (Layer 5 Patent Core)**: Action switching penalty $P_{\text{switch}} = \lambda_{\text{switch}} \sum_i \mathbb{I}(x_{i,a} \neq x_{i,a_{\text{prev}}})$ eliminating decision oscillation in critical infrastructure.
- **Claim 4 (Interchangeable Quantum Engine)**: Selectable solver execution via Qiskit QAOA variational quantum circuits or classical Integer Linear Programming (ILP).
- **Claim 5 (Statutory Provenance)**: Direct legal compliance mapping under DPDP Act 2023, IT Act 2000 (Section 43A), and HIPAA 45 CFR § 164.312(a)(1).
- **Claim 6 (Signal Confidence Gating)**: Confidence-weighted effective threat scoring $s_i \cdot c_i$.
- **Claim 7 (Decision Fidelity Verification)**: Verification metric proving $100.0\%$ Decision Fidelity ($DF\% = 100\%$) with zero broken/illegal actions.
- **Claim 8 (EMA Reinforcement Loop)**: Exponential Moving Average ($\alpha = 0.30$) feedback adaptation across sequential incident rounds.
- **Claim 9 (System Apparatus)**: Hardware apparatus implementing the 9-layer pipeline.
- **Claim 10 (Computer-Readable Medium)**: Non-transitory storage medium storing executable instructions.
