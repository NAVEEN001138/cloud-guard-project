## 1. Core Novelty & Defensibility Statement

The centerpiece of this patent disclosure is **Layer 5 (Adaptive Constraint Generator)**. Using the **Interchangeability Test**:
- If QAOA is replaced with classical ILP or Greedy solvers, the invention remains intact.
- If Federated Learning is replaced with another detector, the invention remains intact.
- **If Layer 5 is removed**, the system collapses into a generic optimization solver.

Layer 5 is the computer-implemented mechanism that dynamically transforms live threat probabilities, confidence tiers, business SLAs, statutory policy rules (e.g. **45 CFR § 164.312(a)(1)** Access Control Safeguards), and historical EMA feedback into a multi-factor Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) constraint matrix.

## 3. Recommended Patent Claim Structure

### Independent Claim 1 (Method Claim)
A computer-implemented method for real-time multi-objective security response optimization in heterogeneous cloud-IoT networks, comprising:
1. Receiving calibrated threat detection probabilities generated by local edge detectors;
2. Aggregating multi-factor context parameters including asset business criticality, service level agreement (SLA) priority, and statutory compliance tags;
3. Dynamically synthesizing a feasibility matrix defining resource-specific physical capability constraints and an action conflict matrix defining mutually exclusive response actions;
4. Formulating a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) objective function $H(x)$ balancing containment effectiveness, operational downtime cost, business impact, and action switching penalties; and
5. Solving the QUBO model via an interchangeable optimization engine to output an actionable, policy-compliant incident response plan.

### Dependent Claims (2 - 6)
- **Claim 2**: The method of claim 1, wherein the optimization engine is selectably configured to execute via Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) circuits or classical Integer Linear Programming (ILP).
- **Claim 3**: The method of claim 1, wherein statutory compliance tags attach explicit regulatory provenance citations, including 45 CFR § 164.312(a)(1) technical access control safeguards.
- **Claim 4**: The method of claim 1, wherein raw continuous threat probabilities are discretized into privacy-preserving operational tiers while maintaining 100% decision fidelity.
- **Claim 5**: The method of claim 1, wherein exponential moving average (EMA) feedback updates containment utility weights across sequential incident rounds without action-switching oscillation.
